About OBA

The Financial Engine of PSAU

The Office of Business Affairs (OBA) manages all revenue-generating activities across Pampanga State Agricultural University — from agricultural produce and rental properties to commercial businesses and non-agricultural services.

Our integrated system centralizes sales, inventory, and financial reporting into one real-time platform, eliminating manual records and ensuring every peso is accounted for.

Real-time Tracking COA-Compliant Reports Role-based Access Audit Trail

Mission

To provide the Office of Business Affairs with a centralized, transparent, and efficient platform for monitoring sales, inventory, and finances across all university business units in compliance with COA and government accounting standards.

Vision

A digitally transformed PSAU where every business unit operates on accurate real-time data, enabling sound decisions, accountable stewardship of public resources, and improved service to the university community.

Why OBA Matters: reunites disjointed sales, inventory, and finance records into a single source of truth — reducing manual work, preventing stockouts and overstocking, and producing audit-ready reports on demand.
